Frequently Asked Quantum Questions

Is Panda ASI really worth 100+ billion?

100+ billion is the scale of the vision we're building toward. There are no shares, tokens or anything for sale here.

Can I buy quantum money or invest?

No. This is a vision site. We don't sell anything or take money, and we don't promise returns. If someone claims to sell “Panda quantum money”, it isn't us.

What is quantum money, honestly?

A real idea from quantum information science (Wiesner, 1970s): banknotes made of quantum states that can't be copied because of the no-cloning theorem. Practical versions are still research.

Does a datacenter really produce more energy than it uses?

Not by the laws of physics we know — it's our dream framing for datacenters paired with local renewables, waste-heat reuse and storage so they give a lot back to their communities.

Where is a superposition payment, in this vision?

Not in one place. It is verifiable everywhere, at any time, and settles when it is used. This is a concept, not a live payment product.

Are the energy drinks for sale?

No. Panda Quantum and Panda ASI drinks are a brand and product concept, not something you can buy today.
